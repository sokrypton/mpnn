Amino-acid bias & omissions

Bias is added to the logits before sampling, so +2 makes a residue roughly e² times more likely. Omit removes it entirely.

Symmetry / tied positions

Comma-separated groups of 1-based positions that must share an identity, e.g. 10+40, 11+41. Tied positions are decoded together and their logits averaged.
