Bias & omissions

Bias is added to the logits before sampling, so +2 makes a residue roughly e² times more likely — it only wins where the model's own preference is weaker than that. Omit removes a letter outright.

Tied positions

Tied positions are decoded together and share one identity. Their logits are averaged, so a group behaves like a single position; the sum mode adds them instead, which sharpens as the group grows.
